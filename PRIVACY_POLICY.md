# Privacy Policy for Jainsansaar

**Last updated:** February 2025

**Developer/Operator:** Jainsansaar  
**App name:** Jainsansaar  
**Package:** com.jainsansaar.jainapp

---

## 1. Introduction

Jainsansaar ("we," "our," or "the app") is a mobile application that connects the Jain community with temples, social features, and cultural content. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Jainsansaar, you agree to this policy.

---

## 2. About Our App

Jainsansaar provides:

- **Account & authentication:** Registration, login, OTP verification, password reset
- **User profiles:** Profile creation and editing, profile pictures, bios, contact details
- **Social features:** Posts (photos and videos), likes, comments, shares, stories
- **Real-time chat:** Direct messaging between users
- **Notifications:** In-app and real-time notifications
- **Temple discovery:** Browse temples, view details, locate temples on map
- **Temple location:** Set or update your temple location using GPS for finding nearby temples
- **Content:** Jeevani, songs, PDFs, videos
- **Communication:** Call temple contact numbers from within the app

---

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide

- **Account:** First name, last name, email, mobile number, date of birth, username, password
- **Profile:** Profile picture, bio, location, city, alternate email, contact number
- **Content:** Posts (photos, videos, captions), comments, chat messages, stories
- **Temple:** Temple name and location when you set your temple

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device:** Device type, OS version, app version
- **Usage:** How you use the app (e.g., features used, pages visited)
- **Network:** Network status (to check connectivity)

### 3.3 Location Information

We collect location data **only when you choose to**:

- Find nearby temples
- Set or update your temple location on the map

We do **not** track your location in the background or without your consent.

## 4. Permissions We Request and Why

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Internet** | Connect to our servers for login, posts, chat, notifications, and content |
| **Network state** | Check if you are online before making requests |
| **Camera** | Take photos and videos for posts and profile pictures |
| **Photos / media (images, video, audio)** | Select photos and videos from your gallery for posts and stories |
| **Storage (read/write)** | Save and access media for posts (on older Android versions) |
| **Location (precise and approximate)** | Find nearby temples and set temple location on the map |
| **Phone calls** | Let you call temple contact numbers directly from the app |
| **Foreground service / Wake lock** | Keep the app responsive for real-time chat and notifications while in use |

We request **only** the permissions needed for the features above. You can deny individual permissions; some features may not work if you do.

## 5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account

## 6. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored on our secure servers.
- Passwords are hashed and not stored in plain text.
- We use HTTPS for data in transit.
- We retain your data for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal and operational purposes.
