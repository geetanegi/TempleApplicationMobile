Privacy Policy for Jainsansaar

Last updated: February 2025

Developer: Jainsansaar | App: Jainsansaar | Package: com.jainsansaar.jainapp

1. Introduction

Jainsansaar ("we," "our," or "the app") is a mobile application that connects the Jain community with temples, social features, and cultural content. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Jainsansaar, you agree to this policy.

2. About Our App

Jainsansaar provides:

  • Account & authentication: Registration, login, OTP verification, password reset
  • User profiles: Profile creation and editing, profile pictures, bios, contact details
  • Social features: Posts (photos and videos), likes, comments, shares, stories
  • Real-time chat: Direct messaging between users
  • Notifications: In-app and real-time notifications
  • Temple discovery: Browse temples, view details, locate temples on map
  • Temple location: Set or update your temple location using GPS for finding nearby temples
  • Content: Jeevani, songs, PDFs, videos
  • Communication: Call temple contact numbers from within the app

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Account: First name, last name, email, mobile number, date of birth, username, password
  • Profile: Profile picture, bio, location, city, alternate email, contact number
  • Content: Posts (photos, videos, captions), comments, chat messages, stories
  • Temple: Temple name and location when you set your temple

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device: Device type, OS version, app version
  • Usage: How you use the app (e.g., features used, pages visited)
  • Network: Network status (to check connectivity)

3.3 Location Information

We collect location data only when you choose to find nearby temples or set your temple location on the map. We do not track your location in the background or without your consent.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile and content to other users (when you choose to share)
  • Provide social features (posts, likes, comments, follows, stories)
  • Enable real-time chat and notifications
  • Show temple listings and your location on the map (when you opt in)
  • Improve the app, fix bugs, and enhance security
  • Send account-related or service-related communications (e.g., password reset, OTP)
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on our secure servers. Passwords are hashed and not stored in plain text. We use HTTPS for data in transit. We retain your data for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal and operational purposes.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data:

  • With other users: Only what you choose to share (profile, posts, comments)
  • With service providers: For hosting, analytics, or support, under strict agreements
  • For legal reasons: When required by law or to protect rights and safety

8. Third-Party Services

We use Google Maps for temple map and location (subject to Google's Privacy Policy), WebSocket/STOMP for real-time chat and notifications, and cloud storage (e.g., AWS S3) for media. These services have their own privacy policies.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You may access, correct, or request deletion of your data. You can update your profile in the app and revoke permissions in device settings. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 12.

10. Children's Privacy

Jainsansaar is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.
