Privacy Policy

Jain Sansaar · Last updated: March 22, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of Jain Sansaar (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Jain Sansaar mobile application (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. For questions, contact us at support@jainsansaar.com.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile. When you register or sign in, we process information you provide such as name, username, email address, phone number, date of birth (if requested), and password (transmitted securely to our servers; we do not store your password in plain text).

Content you submit. The App may allow you to upload or post photos, videos, text, feedback, or other materials (“User Content”) for features such as community posts, reels, stories, or profile images.

Location. If you enable location permissions, we collect approximate or precise device location to show nearby temples, maps, or related features. You can disable location in device settings; some features may not work without it.

Device and technical data. We receive technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, language preference, IP address, and diagnostic or log data needed to operate and secure the service.

Local storage. The App uses on-device storage to keep you signed in, cache preferences, store authentication tokens, remember recent searches where applicable, and improve performance. If you use “Remember me”, your login identifier and password may be stored on your device.

Communications. If you contact support or send feedback, we collect the information you provide.

2. How we use information

We use this information to provide, maintain, and improve the App; authenticate users; personalize content; show maps and location-based information when you opt in; process uploads and community features; respond to support requests; comply with law; and detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues.

3. Sharing

We may share information with service providers (hosting, authentication, email, analytics, support) under confidentiality terms. Mapping services (such as Google Maps) process data under their own policies. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security. Information may be transferred in a merger or acquisition. We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. Real-time features

If the App uses WebSockets or similar technologies for chat, notifications, or live updates, related data is processed to deliver those features.

5. Retention & security

We retain information as long as needed to provide the App and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your choices

You may update profile information in the App, adjust device permissions, or sign out. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export data. Contact us at the email above.

The App is not directed at children under the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries where we or our providers operate, using appropriate safeguards where required.
